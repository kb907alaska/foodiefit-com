---
question: "How do you handle dietary preferences and allergens?"
category: "nutrition"
order: 6
---

We clearly list all ingredients, macros, and major allergens for every meal. While we offer many meals made without gluten, dairy, nuts, or soy, all meals are prepared in a shared commercial facility that processes common allergens. Customers with severe anaphylactic allergies should review our [Nutrition & Allergen Disclaimer](/nutrition-allergen-disclaimer/).

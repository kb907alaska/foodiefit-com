## Information We Collect

Foodie Fit collects information that you voluntarily provide when placing an order, contacting support, or signing up for updates. This includes name, delivery address, email, phone number, and communication preferences.

## How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to fulfill meal orders, coordinate delivery or store pickup, provide customer service updates, and improve our services in Las Vegas.

## No Third-Party Selling

We never sell, rent, or trade customer personal information to third parties.

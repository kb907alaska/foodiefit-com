## Commitment to Accessibility

Foodie Fit is dedicated to providing an accessible website experience for individuals with disabilities. We strive to ensure our web content conforms to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

## Accessibility Features

- **Keyboard Navigation**: All interactive menus, macro filters, and buttons are fully accessible via keyboard (`Tab`, `Enter`, `Space`, `Escape`).
- **Screen Reader Support**: Semantic HTML5 tags, ARIA landmarks, and descriptive text alternatives for meal photography.
- **Color Contrast**: Category tags combine high-contrast colors with clear text and icon indicators.
- **Focus Indicators**: Visible 2px outline focus indicators on all focusable elements.

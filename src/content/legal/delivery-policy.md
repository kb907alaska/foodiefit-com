## Delivery Radius & Cutoff Times

Foodie Fit delivers to residential and commercial addresses in Greater Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, and Centennial Hills.

- **Order Cutoff**: Place your order by **6:00 PM** for delivery as early as tomorrow.
- **Insulated Cold Packaging**: All orders are packaged with food-grade gel ice packs inside insulated thermal bags designed to maintain refrigerated temperature (38°F or below) for up to 6 hours at your doorstep.
- **SMS Notifications**: Receive SMS text updates when your delivery driver is en route and when your package has been delivered.

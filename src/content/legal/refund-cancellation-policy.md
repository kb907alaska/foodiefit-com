## Satisfaction Guarantee

At Foodie Fit, we take pride in culinary quality and freshness. If any meal arrives damaged, incorrectly portioned, or fails to meet our quality standards, please contact support within **48 hours** for a store credit or replacement.

## Cancellation Window

- **One-Time Orders**: Cancellations must be made before the 6:00 PM cutoff time prior to your scheduled delivery date.
- **Subscriptions**: Pause, edit, or cancel your recurring subscription at any time prior to your weekly order lock date.

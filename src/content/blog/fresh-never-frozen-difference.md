When food is frozen, ice crystals break cell walls in meats and vegetables, causing moisture loss and soggy textures upon reheating.

## The Foodie Fit Difference

We cook fresh every day in Las Vegas. Our meals are rapidly chilled in commercial flash-coolers to lock in flavor and safety without ever entering a freezer.

Result: Steak stays tender, broccoli stays crisp, and chicken tastes like a restaurant dish.

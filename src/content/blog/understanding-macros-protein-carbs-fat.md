Macronutrients—protein, carbohydrates, and fats—form the foundation of your body's energy and tissue recovery.

## 1. Protein: The Building Block

Protein builds lean muscle, supports immune function, and keeps you full. Foodie Fit offers **Lean (40g+ protein)** and **Bulk (65g+ protein)** portion sizes across our entire menu.

## 2. Carbohydrates: Fuel for Performance

Carbs provide primary energy for workouts and daily brain activity. We color-code our active meals (Green) and low-carb meals (Red) so you can easily fuel according to your workout schedule.

## 3. Healthy Fats: Hormonal Support

Monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats from olive oil, salmon, and nuts support hormone regulation and brain health.
